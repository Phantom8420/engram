How it works

Engram loads a project's pull requests, reviews, issues and decisions into one FalkorDB graph, then asks it three things.

Who really knows itKnowledge fades over time, so a file's real expert is who worked on it lately, not who touched it once. Exit interviewMeasures what leaves with a person, then asks the questions that save the most of it first. What ifCopies the graph, removes a person or a decision, and shows the code left with nobody to ask.

Tested on real departures: Engram named who took over first 51% of the time on psf/black, against 31% for the busiest person.
